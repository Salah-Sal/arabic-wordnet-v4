---

## Motivation

AWN4 (109,901 synsets) was generated by LLM translation from Open English WordNet 2024. Before investing in expensive LLM-based review, create a cheap automated pre-filter that flags mechanical errors using the Arabic dictionary SQLite database (94,537 entries from المعجم الوسيط and المعجم الكبير) as authoritative ground truth.

No bridge previously existed between AWN4 and any Arabic dictionary resource. This script creates the first connection.

## Method

6 checks per synset, all O(1) dictionary lookups after a single stream-parse of the 72 MB XML:

| # | Flag | Scope | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `LEMMA_NOT_FOUND` | per-lemma | No match in dictionary after normalization |
| 2 | `ALL_LEMMAS_MISSING` | per-synset | Zero dictionary evidence for any lemma |
| 3 | `POS_MISMATCH` | per-lemma | AWN4 POS contradicts dictionary POS |
| 4 | `ROOT_INCONSISTENCY` | per-synset | Lemmas have no shared trilateral root |
| 5 | `DIACRITICS_MISMATCH` | per-lemma | AWN4 vocalization differs from dictionary |
| 6 | `DUPLICATE_SYNSETS` | cross-synset | Different synset IDs share identical normalized lemma sets |

**Normalization:** Strip diacritics, normalize alef variants (أ/إ/آ → ا), normalize alef maqsura (ى → ي), remove tatweel, trailing digits, definite article (ال).

## Results

| Flag | Synsets | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| `ALL_LEMMAS_MISSING` | 79,374 (72.2%) | Expected — DB has only 2 of 37+ dictionaries |
| `ROOT_INCONSISTENCY` | 9,196 (8.4%) | Mostly false positives (synonyms naturally have different roots) |
| `DIACRITICS_MISMATCH` | 4,583 (4.2%) | **Actionable** — real vocalization errors (بَرَّأَ vs بَرَأَ, etc.) |
| `POS_MISMATCH` | 3,915 (3.6%) | Mixed — real errors + masdar/adj DB gaps |
| `DUPLICATE_SYNSETS` | 5,378 groups / 12,496 synsets | **Highest value** — polysemy explosion |

### The Pivotal Discovery: Polysemy Explosion

5,378 groups of synsets sharing identical normalized lemma sets:

| Lemma | Synsets | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| عقد | 18 | 18 noun senses all labeled just "عقد" |
| رفع | 15 | 15 verb senses all labeled just "رفع" |
| رأس | 13 | 13 noun senses all labeled just "رأس" |
| قاعدة | 10 | 10 noun senses |
| سحب | 10 | 10 verb senses |

The LLM translator defaulted to the most common Arabic word for each English sense without adding disambiguating synonyms.

## Findings Summary

1. **DUPLICATE_SYNSETS is the highest-value finding.** 12,496 synsets (11.4% of AWN4) are indistinguishable by lemmas alone. This became the focus of the entire polysemy pipeline.

2. **DIACRITICS_MISMATCH is directly fixable.** 4,583 synsets with real vocalization errors. The dictionary form is authoritative and an automated correction pass is feasible.

3. **ROOT_INCONSISTENCY is informational, not an error.** Synonyms naturally come from different etymological families. Useful as reviewer context, not as a flag.

4. **POS_MISMATCH has three sub-patterns:** real errors, masdar ambiguity (noun vs verb for verbal nouns), and DB coverage gaps (adjectives catalogued as nouns).

5. **Dictionary coverage is 24.9%** (vs the 58.9% possible with all 37 dictionaries). Expanding the DB would improve all downstream experiments.
